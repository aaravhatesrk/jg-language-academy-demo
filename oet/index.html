What the OET Exam Actually Tests

The Occupational English Test, or OET, is the English proficiency test built specifically for healthcare — not general academic or migration English. It’s recognised by health regulators and hospital groups across the UK, Ireland, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, Dubai, Namibia, and Ukraine, among others. Instead of asking you to write an essay about climate change, it puts you in situations you’ll actually face at work: reading a set of case notes, listening to a handover between two colleagues, or writing a referral letter that a real GP would need to act on.

Because of that, generic IELTS-style prep only gets a healthcare candidate partway there. The vocabulary, the letter conventions, and even the pace of the listening extracts are built around clinical settings — which is exactly the gap our OET class for nurses and OET class for doctors are designed to close.

OET Class for Nurses vs. OET Class for Doctors — Why We Split Them

Listening and Reading practice can be shared across professions, but the moment you get to Writing and Speaking, the content has to change. A nursing referral letter reads differently from a medical discharge summary — different register, different level of clinical detail, different things the assessor is actually scoring you on. So our OET preparation for nurses and OET preparation for doctors run as separate content tracks inside the same programme.

OET Format

  • Listening
  • Reading
  • Writing
  • Speaking

listening Tips

  • Listen actively and focus on key details.
  • Understand cultural and contextual meanings.
  • Take structured notes while listening.
  • Practice with real-life community dialogues.

reading Tips


  • Skim the passage first to get the main idea.
  • Focus on keywords in the questions to find answers faster.
  • Understand context clues for unknown words.
  • Read the answer choices carefully to avoid traps.

writing Tips

  • Task 1 (Email Writing): Follow the format (Greeting, Purpose, Details, Closing).
  • Task 2 (Survey Response): Give clear reasons and examples for your choice.
  • Use varied sentence structures and correct grammar.
  • Plan before writing; check for spelling and coherence.

speaking Tips

Format Overview

  • Speak clearly and at a natural pace.
  • Organize answers logically (Introduction, Main Points, Conclusion).
  • Use a mix of vocabulary and sentence structures.
  • If unsure, paraphrase or explain in another way instead of staying silent.

FAQs on the OET Exam and OET Preparation Online

What score do I actually need for nursing or medical registration?
It depends on your regulator, but the NMC (UK), HSE, and most Australian and New Zealand bodies ask for a Grade B (roughly 350) in every one of the four sub-tests — not just an average across them. Check your specific regulator’s current requirement before you book, since these do get revised.
How long does OET preparation online usually take?
Most working nurses and doctors we coach need somewhere between four and eight weeks, depending on their starting Writing and Speaking level and how much time they can realistically put in around shifts.
Is OET coaching online as effective as an in-person class?
For OET specifically, yes — the exam itself moved to allow computer-based sittings, and the coaching that matters most (letter correction, role-play feedback) works just as well over video call as it does in a classroom.
Can nurses and doctors train in the same batch?
Listening and Reading sessions can be shared, but Writing and Speaking practice always runs separately, since the letter types and role-play scenarios are profession-specific.
How is OET scored?
Each of the four sub-tests is scored from 0 to 500 in 10-point increments, which maps onto letter grades from A to E. Writing and Speaking are marked independently by trained assessors against fixed criteria.
Does JG Language Academy book my OET exam for me?
We guide you on registration, test centres, and choosing between the computer-based and paper-based formats, but you book directly through the official OET site once your mock scores show you’re ready.
