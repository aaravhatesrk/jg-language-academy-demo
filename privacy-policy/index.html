5. The AI support assistant (specific disclosure)

Because AI-assisted chat raises specific privacy considerations, we disclose the following about how the Chatbot works:

  • On-device storage: the Chatbot stores a randomly generated session identifier and your recent conversation in your browser so the conversation stays coherent across pages during your visit. This is not a tracking cookie and is not linked to your identity; it is cleared when you close the site tab or after a period of inactivity.
  • Processing by AI providers: the text of your messages and recent conversation turns is transmitted to third-party large-language-model (“LLM”) providers to generate a reply (see Section 7). Please do not enter sensitive personal information (such as identity numbers, financial details, or health information) into the Chatbot.
  • Redaction before logging: before a conversation is saved to our internal support log, the system automatically masks email addresses and phone numbers that appear in the text (replacing them with “[redacted]”), other than our own published contact details.
  • Requesting a callback (optional): if you ask the Chatbot to have someone call you back, it will ask for your name and phone number (and, optionally, your course of interest and a preferred time). This is entirely voluntary — you provide these details only if you want us to contact you. When you do, we use them to arrange the callback: the details are emailed to our team and recorded in our internal leads log (a private Google Sheet) so we can follow up. We rely on your consent for this, and you can ask us to delete these details at any time (see Section 11). The automatic redaction described above does not apply to details you deliberately submit for a callback, since their whole purpose is for us to contact you.
  • No automated decisions: the Chatbot provides information only and does not make decisions about you.

11. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act (individuals in India), you have the right to: obtain a summary of your personal data and the processing activities; seek correction, completion, updating and erasure of your personal data; readily available grievance redressal; and nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.

Under the GDPR (individuals in the EEA/UK), you additionally have the right to: access your data; rectification; erasure; restriction of processing; data portability; to object to processing based on legitimate interests; to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting prior lawful processing); and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 16. We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law and may first need to verify your identity. Exercising these rights is free of charge, save where the law permits a reasonable fee for manifestly unfounded or excessive requests.

12. Children’s data

We offer programs intended for children (for example, Spoken English for ages 5–12). Under the DPDP Act, the processing of a child’s personal data requires the verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian, and we do not undertake tracking, behavioural monitoring, or targeted advertising directed at children. Enrolments and enquiries relating to a minor should be made by a parent or guardian. Under the GDPR, where applicable, we rely on parental consent for users below the applicable age of digital consent. As a safeguard, the Chatbot is designed not to collect contact details from anyone who indicates they are under 18 — in that case it asks a parent or guardian to make the request and provide their own details instead. Callback/enrolment requests concerning a minor should therefore be made by a parent or guardian, who provides their own contact details. Please do not permit a child to submit personal information through the Chatbot without parental/guardian involvement.
